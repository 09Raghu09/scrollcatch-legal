# ScrollCatch Publisher — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 14, 2026

## 1. Overview

ScrollCatch Publisher is a privately operated tool used to prepare, review, and manage short-form social media videos. This policy explains how information may be handled when the operator uses the tool and connects social media accounts.

## 2. Information that may be processed

Depending on the integrations enabled, the tool may process:

- Social media account identifiers and authorization information needed to connect to platform APIs.
- Video files, cover images, and related publishing information prepared by the operator.
- API responses and technical error information needed to diagnose publishing problems.

The tool is intended to use only the information needed to operate its configured publishing workflows.

## 3. Platform authorization

When an account is connected, the relevant platform may provide authorization tokens and account information. These are used to perform the actions authorized by the account holder. Platform providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

## 4. How information is used

Information may be used to prepare and validate videos, maintain approval status, request authorized publishing actions, and troubleshoot technical failures.

The workflow is designed to require the operator's approval before publishing a video.

## 5. Storage and security

The operator is responsible for securing the computer, configuration files, credentials, authorization tokens, and any backups used by the tool. Access to stored information should be limited to what is necessary.

No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Sharing and third-party services

Information may be transmitted to a social media platform when the operator uses its API. The tool does not claim to control how third-party platforms store or use information after receiving it.

Information may also be processed by infrastructure or software services that the operator actually configures.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Information should be retained only as long as needed for the configured workflow, troubleshooting, or applicable legal obligations. The operator can remove local files and revoke platform authorization where supported.

## 8. Your choices

The account holder can disconnect integrations, revoke authorization through the relevant platform, and request deletion of information where applicable.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the tool or its integrations change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised version is published.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through the official ScrollCatch social media pages.
